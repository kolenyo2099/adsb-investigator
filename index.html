How to use this tool →

Gaps are the finding, not an inferred destination. Envelope radii assume 850 km/h cruise and 0–2h ground stops.

Scans are precomputed offline (scripts/scan_area.py) — the browser can't reach adsb.lol's heatmap files directly (no CORS). Red = US-registered; grey = registration country not yet resolved.

Loads the trace or scan you last viewed into a pandas DataFrame called df, in-browser (Pyodide, ~10 MB, loaded on demand). Nothing leaves your machine.
